# Arapçagah Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 5, 2026_

## Privacy Policy

Arapçagah respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

### Information We Collect

When you use Arapçagah, we may collect:

- Your Google account name and email address (when you sign in)
- Learning progress
- Completed lessons
- Favorite words
- Application crash reports
- Basic usage information

### Why We Collect This Information

We use this information to:

- Authenticate your account
- Synchronize your learning progress across devices
- Save your favorite words
- Improve application stability
- Fix errors

### Third-Party Services

Arapçagah uses:

- Google Sign-In
- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Firebase Crashlytics

These services have their own privacy policies.

### Data Security

Your information is transmitted securely and stored using Google's Firebase infrastructure.
